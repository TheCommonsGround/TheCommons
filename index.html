How this prototype maps to the real system
1. CollectorsEvents are now real, added by hand via the admin page and stored in Netlify Blobs. Automated collectors (APIs, RSS, calendars) are the next step, not yet built.
2. NormalizationEach source's format gets converted into the same shape: title, org, date, time, location, tags.
3. Dedup + geocodingDuplicate listings get merged; addresses get turned into coordinates so radius search works for real.
4. Search + AIThe search box on the home screen already calls Claude live to turn a sentence into filters — that part isn't mocked.
5. Tree plantingEvery RSVP, from every visitor, counts toward one shared total (stored in Netlify Blobs). Every 50th RSVP funds one real tree via the Ecologi API, once ECOLOGI_API_KEY is set.

The Common Ground Project

The Northeast's forests are one of the great regrowth stories anywhere — a region that was once 80% deforested is now dense with young forest that sequesters carbon at some of the highest rates in the world. Most people living here don't know that. The Common Ground Project exists to help people see the place they live: how the forest around them works, why it matters, and how to take part in caring for it.

People protect what they know. They care for what they love.

Two pillars, one campaign

The Commons — the site you're using right now — is the "Website" pillar, alongside "Media." (Panel and Education programming lives under Standing Trees, a partner nonprofit — their work will surface as content here, but sits outside this campaign's own structure.)

01

Media

Help people see what's around them.

A social and YouTube campaign sharing science, stories, and people — countering confusion and building real investment in this place.

02

Website — The Commons

Help people find a way to participate.

This site: one search across events, education, media, and eventually jobs, internships, and community gardens — the pathway from awareness to action.

Media & articles

Content from the Media pillar lands here as it's produced — Instagram, TikTok, and long-form YouTube pieces, plus written stories.
